**AETHER 2.2** fixes a crash at launch, syncs your YouTube Music playlists, and sorts a playlist's songs.

### Fixed
- **AETHER closed as it opened** for anyone whose history held a song with no artist named (an upload, or a song kept under 2.0). Profile now copes, and those plays count for their artist again where the phone knows who it was.
- **Songs with no artist:** some of YouTube Music's shelves and suggestions leave the artist out. AETHER now finds it a moment after the song starts, so Now Playing, the notification and Profile name who made it, with no break in the sound.
- **Cover films** played a middling copy of the film. Every film now plays the richest copy your phone can decode.

### New
- **Your YouTube Music playlists**, once you're signed in, are in AETHER's Library and stay up to date. Signing out takes away what the sync brought; songs you kept stay on the phone.
- **Sort a playlist** by your order, title, artist, album, when songs were added (your own playlists) or length. Each playlist keeps its own choice.
- **Sharper song films:** a song's cover film on Now Playing plays Apple's finest copy, up to 2160 square or 2048 x 2732 tall, on Wi-Fi and mobile data. The data saver still loads no film.

### Installing
Needs Android 10 or later. AETHER 2.2 installs over any earlier AETHER and keeps your library; AETHER on your phone offers it by itself.
