**AETHER v2** is about the words and the way songs meet: lyrics from seven libraries drawn three ways, crossfade, better copies found while a song plays, and your offline music as albums.

### What's new
- **Lyrics from seven libraries:** lrc.red, BiniLyrics, BetterLyrics, LyricsPlus, Unison, LRCLIB and KuGou, asked together, the best answer kept, word by word where a library has it. A song on your phone brings its own words first: its .lrc beside it, or the words in its file.
- **Verse:** the song as a page that's sung, each word lighting syllable by syllable behind a soft edge, held notes swelling letter by letter and glowing, the answering vocal opening beneath its line, duets from both sides.
- **Words in Latin letters:** Korean, Chinese, Japanese kana and other scripts romanised on the phone, beneath each line.
- **Crossfade:** 2 to 12 seconds between songs, never between an album's songs that run into each other.
- **Your sources, at their best:** for a song from YouTube Music, your own sources are asked for the very same recording, lossless where they have it. Found while the song plays, it goes over to that copy mid-song, lined up by its sound and handed over in a crossfade you shouldn't hear.
- **Music videos:** a video plays as video in place of its cover, at 720p, its pictures following the music.
- **Albums on Listen:** the music on your phone as albums, and the songs you've kept as albums, two shelves of their own.
- **Kept songs in your Music folder:** every kept song is a file in Music/AETHER, the same audio that streamed, with its cover and words in its tags.
- **Full screen:** Android's bars hidden, in Settings under Feel.
- **Updates in the app:** a newer AETHER is offered on a sheet, checked against the release and handed to Android to install.

### Better
- A song that fails mid-play is tried again from where it stopped, from another source, app or format, before you're told.
- Nothing is written over covers any more.
- Listen's figures change only once the page has been still for a few seconds.

### Installing
Needs Android 10 or later. AETHER v2 installs over v1 and keeps your library.
