**AETHER 1.0** is the first release: a premium music player for Android, built around the sound and unlike anything you've used.

### What's in it
- **All of YouTube Music, and your own:** the whole catalogue, as a guest or signed in on Google's own page. Your FLAC, ALAC and WAV files, folder by folder. Songs and whole playlists kept for offline. Your own server, added by address.
- **An honest signal path:** "Lossless", "Hi-Res" and "Bit-perfect" appear only when measured or verified. Bit-perfect to a USB DAC on Android 14 and later.
- **An equaliser that knows your headphones:** measured corrections for 39 models, including AirPods Max and Pro, Sennheiser, Sony, Marshall and Bose. Seven tunings of our own, and profiles imported from AutoEq, squig.link, Equalizer APO or Wavelet.
- **Statues that rearrange themselves:** behind Listen, twelve figures from antiquity in halftone, every dot flying across to become the next god.
- **Now Playing as an instrument:** a dial with a red needle, and the album's own film in colour where it has one, fading in an arc round the controls.
- **Metal you can send:** your listening on a black or brushed silver charge card, and any song on a card of its own, at story size.
- **A song going into the phone:** keep one and its cover sinks into the foot of the screen with a ripple through the whole display.
- **Lyrics drawn by hand:** words as a constellation, or the whole song as one unbroken line.
- **Zero nonsense:** no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no account and no server of ours.

### Installing
Needs Android 10 or later. Open the APK on your phone, and Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file manager.

If you have an earlier test build of AETHER, uninstall it first: this is the first build signed with AETHER's own release key. From here on, new versions install over old ones and keep your library.
