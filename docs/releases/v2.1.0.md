**AETHER 2.1** fixes what 2.0 got wrong, and tidies playlists.

### Fixed
- **Kept songs show again:** their name, artist and cover on Now Playing, the mini player, the notification and Samsung's Now Bar, offline too. A queue saved while one played is mended.
- **"See all" that opened empty:** Trending, and every artist's Top songs and Videos, now show all their songs and videos, two to a row.
- **Cover films:** every song's film is back, at its best quality from the first frame, portrait films included.
- **Crashes:** two ways a page could close the app are gone. If AETHER ever does close by itself, Settings ▸ About keeps what went wrong, on your phone only, for you to share if you like.

### Playlists
- Rename, Edit songs and Delete live in a menu at the top; Delete asks first.
- A search key opens into a field and finds songs as you type.
- A small arrow marks every song kept on the phone.
- Playlists you saved, or kept songs from, are in your Library; offline, one with nothing kept is greyed out and one with kept songs opens on them.
- Playlists no longer keep new songs by themselves.

### Now Playing
- One video key: on, a song shows its film and a music video plays as itself; off, just the cover.
- The print wears its tattoo again; its style is chosen in Settings.
- One download disc at the foot, the number coming down written on its cover, clear of the bars.

### Installing
Needs Android 10 or later. AETHER 2.1 installs over 2.0 and keeps your library; AETHER on your phone offers it by itself.
