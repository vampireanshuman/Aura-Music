**AETHER 2.3** fixes "The music service didn't answer", handles your own sources the way BitChord does, and puts AETHER's mark on the player's notification.

### Fixed
- **"The music service didn't answer" on every page,** for phones set to a language or country YouTube Music doesn't take (Santali, Konkani, Kashmiri, Sindhi, Dogri, Maithili, Bodo, Manipuri, Yiddish; China, Iran, Afghanistan, Myanmar and a few more), signed in or not. AETHER now finds out which it was and asks without it.
- **What's offered follows where you are,** not where your phone is set: AETHER sends the country YouTube Music finds you in, as its website does.
- **An ended sign-in says so,** instead of saying a page needs an account.
- **Kept songs in file managers:** songs kept as M4A now carry their cover, name, artist, album and words; a kept song's cover is fetched again if the first try fails.
- **Your own sources:** one that didn't answer when AETHER started is asked again when the connection comes back and every two minutes (and Settings has Try again); one that refuses says why, in its own words, and rests so songs don't wait on it.

### New
- **Sources handled as BitChord's host handles them:** a song's stream is asked for first, with its search row's own address as the fallback, songs from a source show their covers, and a source that asks to be used only with a lossless output (wired or USB headphones, HDMI, or Bluetooth on LDAC, LHDC or aptX Lossless) waits until one is connected. To tell which codec your Bluetooth headphones use, AETHER asks for Nearby devices and, on Android 14 and later, Android's one-time permission to know those headphones: both from that source's row in Settings, and only when it matters.
- **AETHER's mark** in the status bar and on the player's notification, in place of the play symbol.
- **An artist's Albums and Singles & EPs** run down the page two to a row, as videos do, the page named for the shelf with the artist above it.

### Installing
Needs Android 10 or later. AETHER 2.3 installs over any earlier AETHER and keeps your library; AETHER on your phone offers it by itself.
